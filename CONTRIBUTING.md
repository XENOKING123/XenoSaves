# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in XenoSaves.

This repository is a **fork of [earthonion/GarlicTransfer](https://github.com/earthonion/GarlicTransfer)**
with XenoSaves added on top. Please keep the two separate:

- **GarlicTransfer** parts belong to earthonion. Don't change them here — send improvements
  upstream to earthonion's repository.
- **XenoSaves** is maintained by XENO KING. The application source lives in a separate
  repository; this repo holds the public documentation, branding, and releases.

## Ways to help
- **Report a bug** — open an issue with your Windows version, what you did, and what happened.
- **Suggest a feature** — open an issue describing the idea and why it helps.
- **Share saves** — use the in-app community "Share a save"; an admin reviews it.
- **Security** — report privately (see `XenoSaves/SECURITY.md`), not in a public issue.

## Please don't
- Post cracked, pirated, or paid content.
- Upload someone else's save as your own.
- Share account tokens or private keys.

Be kind. This is a community.
